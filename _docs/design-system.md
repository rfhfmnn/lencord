Typography
- Primary / Display Font: `Plus Jakarta Sans` (titles, body text, and numeric display).
- Monospace Font: Monospaced font for CUIT/CUIL, CBUs/CVUs, transaction IDs, and cryptographic hashes.
- Casing & Tone: Spanish standard sentence case ("tipo oración"). Avoid capitalizing every word in headings.

Color Palette & Tokens
- Primary (Action): Deep Navy (`#0A2540` / `#1E40AF`) with cobalt accents for primary CTA buttons and active states.
- Backgrounds: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) and Light Neutral Grey (`#F8FAFC`).
- Surfaces & Cards: White (`#FFFFFF`) with subtle borders in `#E2E8F0`.
- Text: Primary text `#0F172A`, Secondary text `#64748B`.

Risk Rating Badges (Semáforo de Riesgo)
- Tier A:
  - Background: `#D1FAE5` (soft green)
  - Text: `#065F46` (emerald)
- Tier B:
  - Background: `#FEF3C7` (soft amber)
  - Text: `#92400E` (warm brown)
- Tier C:
  - Background: `#FFEDD5` (soft orange)
  - Text: `#9A3412` (terracotta)

Base UI Primitives & Components
- Buttons: Primary (Navy), Secondary (Bordered/Ghost).
- Cards: Marketplace loan items with progress bars (funding %), risk badges, and deadline indicators.
- Navigation: Sticky top navigation bar and compliance footer with legal disclaimers (BCRA, UIF).
- Forms: Multi-step wizard layout with clear step indicators and validation highlights.
